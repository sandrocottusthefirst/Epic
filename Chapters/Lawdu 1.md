They passed the last town before the border just as it was getting dark, and the sunset released the wild animals from their stupor. The bushes whispered with the sounds of their inhabitants. The bandits and thieves were probably getting ready for their day's work. _We should have stopped at that town._ He would not say it, of course, lest he be viewed as a coward. He was a green boy as is. "Shouldn't we have stayed the night at the town?" It was another new recruit. "What's your name, boy?" The warden turned. He hadn't talked the whole way. He hadn't even introduced himself. "/Archer-boy/".
        "/Archer-boy/. What do you fear so much that you would waste an entire night to avoid it?" 
"I don't know. Thieves, bandits. The like." The rest of them laughed. /Lawdu/ was silent. "Your duty is to protect the townsfolk from those thieves and bandits." He scowled as the recruit spoke again. "Is this what it's come to out here? That we catch thieves and bandits instead of crushing the barbarian armies?" 
	The recruits laughed once again. The warden frowned even more and said, "I can see why the old geezer wanted you rich green runts to serve at the border." He turned and muttered to himself, "The little girls want to slay some dragons, it seems." He let out a grunt, which /Lawdu/ later realised was a chuckle. /Archer-boy/'s forehead tensed at the comment. He was about to get off his horse, but /Lawdu/ held him down. "I've heard about this guy at the Academy. He's the warden at the /Riverland/ border. Better not to piss him off." /Archer-boy/ swatted his hand off. /Lawdu/ didn't know how many of the stories were true, but if any of them were, he was a man to be feared. 
"Make camp right here. We leave in the next two hours. Prepare accordingly." 
	"Wouldn't the smoke from the fire invite bandits?" It seemed /Archer-boy/ was not one to keep quiet.
