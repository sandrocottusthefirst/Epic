They passed the last town before the border just as it was getting dark, and the sunset released the wild animals from their stupor. The bushes whispered with the sounds of their inhabitants. The bandits and thieves were probably getting ready for their day's work. _We should have stopped at that town._ He would not say it, of course, lest he be viewed as a coward. He was a green boy as is. "Shouldn't we have stayed the night at the town?" It was another new recruit. "What's your name, boy?" The warden turned. He hadn't talked the whole way. He hadn't even introduced himself. "*Archer-boy*".

"*Archer-boy*. What do you fear so much that you would waste an entire night to avoid it?" 
"I don't know. Thieves, bandits. The like." The rest of them laughed. *Lawdu* was silent. "You're supposed to protect the townsfolk from those thieves and bandits." He scowled as the recruit spoke again. "Is this what it's come to out here? That we catch thieves and bandits instead of crushing the barbarian armies? Maybe my father was right, the borders have gone soft living so close to the hordes." 

The recruits laughed once again. The warden frowned even more and said, "I can see why the old geezer wanted you rich green runts to serve at the border." He turned and muttered to himself, "It seems the little girls want to slay some dragons." He let out a grunt, which *Lawdu* later realised was a chuckle. *Archer-boy*'s forehead tensed at the comment. He was about to get off his horse, but *Lawdu* held him down. "I've heard about him at the Academy. The man is a beast in human flesh. Better not to piss him off." *Archer-boy* swatted his hand off. "Alright, alright." *Lawdu* didn't know how many of the stories were true, but if any of them were, he was a man to be feared. His missing eye and the scar that ran across it were enough to validate half of them. Word was that an illness had caught his eye during a campaign, and after the medics told him nothing could be done about it, he burned his dagger and stabbed it right out himself, no hesitation. One doesn't have to wonder too much what else such a man could be capable of.
"Make camp right here. We leave in the next two hours. Prepare accordingly." 

"Wouldn't the smoke from the fire invite bandits?" It seemed *Archer-boy* was not one to keep quiet. "If you're lucky, they might just show up. What's a man like you fear about mere bandits?" 
    "Nothing, just making sure." The frown never left *Archer-boy*'s
face. The warden grunted again. "Get to work, kid." 
  He got on his horse and just trotted off to gods know where. 
*Lawdu* looked around in confusion as the four soldiers that had come with the warden to escort the recruits followed him. "What just happened?"

The recruits all gathered in one place, trying to decide a course of action. Some recruit started speaking, "This is clearly a test. Making a camp here would be a tactical mistake. We should stay put and make no noise until they return." Murmurs of approval were heard among the recruits. 
    *Why is everyone agreeing with this?* 
*Archer-boy* spoke next, "Of course you all agree. You're idiots and cowards, every single one of you. You think our first lesson on the border is going to be 'disobey the direct orders of your superior'? You people will get us all killed before we even reach the border." This immediately caused a ruckus, there was heckling and name-calling, and it might have gone out of control had *Lawdu* not grabbed their attention by banging his sword against his shield. 

"As much as I hate to agree with him, *Archer-boy* is right here. The military manuals say that a subordinate may question a superior's decision, but never override it. It is also a stupid lesson to teach rookies to disobey orders on the basis of their own conceptions. We have no choice but to build the camp and await further orders. We can, however set up a defensive formation and prepare for any attacks, which is probably the real test anyway."

Fortunately for *Lawdu*, nobody objected. "It seems not everyone here is a complete imbecile," *Archer-boy* said, grinning. "We should do a head count first, then set up scouting and signalling positions on trees as we were taught. One division will set up campfires, another will be up on the trees keeping watch, and another will standby armed and ready in case of attack." The head count turned up three people unaccounted for. Best case, they were split off during the commotion and would find their way back once the campfire started burning. But there was no real way to look for them. The one who had spoken earlier said, "I'll take the campfire division. Those who want to set up the camp, come with me. My name is *pussy* of *pussyland*." 
    "Of course", *Archer-boy* muttered to himself, just loud enough for others to hear.
"I'll take the armed division. Those who have balls can come with me."
The rest of them quietly followed *Lawdu*.
